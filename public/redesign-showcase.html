Key Improvements

What Changed

1

TrainingCard Component

Replaced blue accents (#0071e3) with emerald green (#1e9953), swapped emoji icons for proper SVG icons, updated to white card background with design system shadows and refined hover states.

2

ClientsMarquee Component

Improved typography hierarchy with eyebrow labels, enhanced logo opacity from 0.75 to 0.85, added sophisticated hover states with brightness filters, and increased section padding from 80px to 120px per design system.

3

GradientCard Component

Transformed from dark background (#011509) to white aesthetic (#ffffff) while preserving advanced 3D rotation effects. Updated all colors, shadows, and glow effects to work with white card design.

4

Design System Foundation

Created comprehensive DESIGN.md and PRODUCT.md specifications including typography scale, color tokens, spacing rules (8px base grid, 120px sections), motion easing curves, and component specifications.
