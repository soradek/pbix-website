# pbix.pl Brand & Product Context

## Register
**brand** — Marketing site for training courses. Design IS the product. Strategic focus: positioning, premium perception, credibility.

## Brand Identity

### Users
- **Primary:** Business analysts, data analysts, finance professionals (age 25–45)
- **Secondary:** Managers needing Power BI expertise, Excel power users, SQL developers
- **Pain point:** Overwhelmed by generic training; want practical, no-BS instruction from someone who actually works in the field

### Product Purpose
Digital storefront for **corporate training in data analytics tools** (Power BI, Excel, SQL, VBA). 
- Sell training courses
- Establish Radosław as credible, trustworthy trainer
- Convert visitors into course customers or direct inquiries

### Brand Tone
- **Professional** but approachable; zero corporate fluff
- **Practical, direct** — cut the "synergy" speak
- **No hype, just results** — case studies and real examples beat promises
- **Confident** — seven years, 4,500+ trained professionals, MCT certified

### Brand Core Values
1. **Practical over theoretical** — every lesson applies immediately
2. **Respect your time** — no wasted words, no filler modules
3. **Real world examples** — learn from actual business data, not fake datasets
4. **Zero pressure** — ask anything; this is training, not a performance test

### Anti-References
- Generic SaaS landing pages (white/gray + blue button)
- Course mills with hundreds of testimonials (looks fake)
- Overly corporate trainer branding (stiff, distant)
- "Industry leader" / "disrupt" / "paradigm shift" copy
- Stock photos of diverse teams looking at a laptop

## Visual Brand Direction

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Emerald green #1e9953 (warm, professional, growth-oriented)
- **Dark green:** #0a4d2c (depth, grounding)
- **Light green:** #d4f1e4 (backgrounds, accents)
- **Neutrals:** Warm grays (tinted slightly toward the green)
  - Off-black: #1d1d1f
  - Dark gray: #6e6e73
  - Light gray: #f5f5f7

### Aesthetic Direction
- Clean, minimal (not sparse)
- High contrast (legibility over decoration)
- Abundant whitespace (breathing room)
- Subtle textures, not glossy
- Premium feel without being ostentatious

### Typography
- **Display:** Geometric sans-serif, bold weights for impact
- **Body:** Clean, readable sans-serif (Inter, Geist, or Satoshi)
- **Scale:** Conservative — no oversized headlines unless earned by content

## Messaging Pillars
1. **Practical Training** — Learn what you'll use tomorrow
2. **Expert Instruction** — From someone who lives this daily
3. **Business-First** — Every lesson tied to real decisions and outcomes
4. **Supportive Environment** — No gatekeeping, no shame in asking

## Accessibility & Performance
- WCAG AA minimum (AAA preferred)
- Fast load times (Core Web Vitals green)
- Mobile-first, responsive across all devices
- No flashing, no auto-playing media
